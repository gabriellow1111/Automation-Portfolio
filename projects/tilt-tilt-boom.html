Tilt Tilt Boom

Play on DigiPen Gallery

A 2D physics puzzle game built on a proprietary C++ engine[cite: 34, 36]. Players assume the role of an operative defusing explosive devices by clicking and dragging handles to tilt the circular environment, maneuvering an unstable, reactive atom through precision obstacles toward the exhaust port before detonation.

Core Interaction & Physics

Placeholder 01

Figure 1: Mouse cursor drag manipulation of external bomb handles; dynamic rotational transformation updating gravity vectors on the reactive atom.

Engineering the Dynamic Entity Pipeline

Serving as the Physics Champion on the project, the primary challenge was delivering responsive collision dynamics while avoiding rigid, hardcoded scripts for level hazards. Rather than programming bespoke handling loops for each mechanic, an extensible entity-interaction pipeline was engineered directly into the core engine[cite: 36].

Custom Kinematics & Collision

AABB checks, sweep tests, and circle-to-segment collision resolution routines calculated in C++ to preserve player momentum and produce tactile game feel[cite: 36, 37].

Modular Interaction Architecture

Polymorphic trigger volumes and component-based response hooks allowed designers to deploy diverse hazards without re-engineering base physics loops.
